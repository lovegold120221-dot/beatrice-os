Release builds require a `key.properties` file at `flutter/android/key.properties` pointing to `upload-keystore.jks`; signing is applied through the `release` signingConfig. ProGuard/R8 shrinking is enabled for release via `proguard-rules.pro`. The app declares explicit `<queries>` for Termux, YouTube, Gmail, Chrome, Maps, Messaging, WhatsApp, and WhatsApp Business to satisfy Android 11+ package visibility.
