Requires environment variables per provider: `NEXT_PUBLIC_HF_TOKEN`/`HF_TOKEN` for Flux, `NEXT_PUBLIC_GEMINI_API_KEY`/`GEMINI_API_KEY` for Gemini, and `NEXT_PUBLIC_OLLAMA_BASE_URL`/`OLLAMA_BASE_URL` plus `NEXT_PUBLIC_OLLAMA_MODEL`/`OLLAMA_MODEL` for local/hosted Ollama. Supabase tables `memories` (with `user_id,content,category,created_at,last_accessed` and a `user_id,content` conflict target) and `device_profiles` must exist before use.
